Privacy Policy

Effective: February 9, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect on our website (hrcompliancecompass.com) and in our app (app.hrcompliancecompass.com), why we collect it, and the choices you have.

HR Compliance Compass (“HRCC”) is operated by CompassLine LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Questions? Email support@hrcompliancecompass.com.

Not legal advice: HRCC provides general guidance and tools. For legal advice, please consult a qualified attorney.

What we collect

  • Your account: when you create a free account, your email address and sign-in details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Details you choose to add: your first name, home state, the states you work in, company name, number of employees, industry, and the topics you want help with.
  • Plan and billing status: whether you’re on the free or Pro plan, and the customer ID our payment processor gives us. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Forms you submit: your name, email and message when you contact us or request a free guide.
  • How you use the site and app: pages viewed, clicks, and key actions such as signing up, upgrading or cancelling (see “Analytics and advertising tools” below).
  • How you found us: the website or campaign that referred you, saved in your browser so we can see which channels help people.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, and error logs needed to run and secure the service.

Note: We do not ask for employee Social Security numbers, bank details, payroll files, or other sensitive employee personal data.

Analytics and advertising tools

We use the following tools. Each sets cookies or similar technologies in your browser.

  • Google Analytics 4 (website and app): counts visits, pages viewed and where visitors come from, and records key actions such as signing up, clicking to upgrade, buying Pro and cancelling Pro. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Microsoft Clarity (website and app): records how visitors use our pages, such as clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, as session replays and heatmaps, so we can find and fix confusing pages. On our website, Clarity hides anything you type into forms; replays show our page text and where people click and scroll. In the app, Clarity hides all text, including anything you type. Learn more in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Meta Pixel (website only): tells us whether our Facebook and Instagram ads lead to visits and sign-ups, and lets Meta show our ads to people who have visited our site. You can control this in your Facebook ad preferences.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site and app still work without analytics cookies.

Affiliate links

Our Recommended Tools page includes links marked “Affiliate link.” When you click one, our affiliate network, Awin, sets a cookie so the company can credit us if you buy. Awin handles that data under its own privacy policy. Affiliate links never affect our HR Directory or our law content.

Emails

  • Account emails: account confirmation and password reset emails, and payment receipts from Stripe.
  • Welcome emails: a welcome email when you sign up and a follow-up about upcoming law changes about a week later.
  • Guide emails: the free guide you requested.

Every non-essential email has a one-click unsubscribe link, or you can email us to stop them.

How we use information

  • Provide the app, show the laws for your state, and manage your plan.
  • Respond to your requests and support questions.
  • Send the emails described above.
  • Understand what’s helpful and improve the site and app (debugging, performance, usability and content).
  • Measure whether our ads and outreach work.
  • Keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and enforce our Terms.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • We do not use your information to train public AI models.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run HRCC:

  • Hosting and forms: Netlify hosts our website and receives form submissions.
  • Accounts and data storage: Supabase stores your account and app data.
  • Payments: Stripe processes Pro subscriptions and payments.
  • Email delivery: Resend sends our emails.
  • Analytics and advertising: Google, Microsoft and Meta, as described above.
  • Affiliate network: Awin, when you click an affiliate link.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transaction (such as a merger or asset sale).

Retention

We keep your account information while your account is open. After you close your account or ask us to delete it, we delete it unless we must keep it for legal, tax or billing reasons. Form submissions are kept only as long as needed to respond.

Your choices

  • Update your details anytime on the My Account page in the app.
  • Cancel Pro anytime from the My Account page.
  • Unsubscribe from non-essential emails with the link in any email.
  • Opt out of analytics and ad tracking using the links above or your browser settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account by emailing support@hrcompliancecompass.com.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls. No online system is 100% secure, but we work to reduce risk.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we’ll update the “Last updated” date above.
