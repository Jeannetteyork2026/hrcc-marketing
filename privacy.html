Privacy Policy

Effective: February 9, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

HR Compliance Compass (“HRCC”) is operated by CompassLine LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy covers our website and app. Questions? Email support@hrcompliancecompass.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email, sign-in details, and anything you add, such as your name, state and company details.
  • Forms: what you send us when you contact us or request a guide.
  • Usage data: pages visited, clicks, and basic device and browser information.

We do not ask for employee Social Security numbers, bank details, payroll files, or other sensitive employee data.

How we use it

To run the app and your account, respond to you, send account and welcome emails, improve the site, measure our ads, and keep the service secure.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity (session recordings and heatmaps) and the Meta Pixel to understand how the site and app are used and how our ads perform. Links marked “Affiliate link” use a cookie from our affiliate network, Awin. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s opt-out add-on.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers that help us run HRCC, such as hosting, payments (Stripe), email delivery and the analytics tools above, or when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can update your details or cancel Pro from the My Account page, and unsubscribe from emails with the link in any email. To get a copy of your information or delete your account, email support@hrcompliancecompass.com.

Retention and security

We keep your information while your account is open and delete it when you ask, unless we must keep it for legal or billing reasons. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system is 100% secure.

Children

HRCC is for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we’ll update the “Last updated” date above.
